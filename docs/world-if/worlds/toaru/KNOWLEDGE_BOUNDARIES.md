# Toaru Knowledge Boundaries — World IF v1

A true canon fact is not automatically character knowledge. Ask whether it is public, personally witnessed, organizationally disclosed, revealed in this IF, or merely guessed.

- Ordinary Academy City students plausibly know schools, public institutions, ability levels, famous Level 5s and festival information; not real magic organizations, Aleister's deeper plans, or unrestricted dark-side secrets.
- Judgment gets relevant local case/public-order information, not unrestricted occult/dark-side intelligence.
- Anti-Skill gets official incident/public-security information, not automatic Board secrets.
- Magic operatives know relevant magic/church/mission facts, not every Academy City experiment.
- Touma knows what he experienced on both sides, not either side's complete structure; at this baseline he hides his memory loss from Index.
- Index has vast grimoire/magic knowledge and weak science/technology understanding.
- Mikoto has strong science-side and personal Sisters knowledge; baseline magic knowledge is poor.
- Kuroko has Judgment/personal knowledge and may investigate, but cannot magically infer Mikoto's private secrets.
- Shokuhou can gather exceptional human information through her faction/ability only with a causal access path; she is not omniscient.
- Accelerator has deep access to some research/dark-side reality but not every Board/magic secret.
- Tsuchimikado has unusually broad cross-side information and strategically withholds it.
- Stiyl/Kanzaki are strong magic-side sources; science secrets require actual intelligence.

Narrator may maintain hidden truth for consistency, but should prefer observable effects over free exposition when the focal characters cannot know the secret.
