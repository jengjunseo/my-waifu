## Academy City
A cutting-edge science/education city in western Tokyo. Roughly 80% of its population is students, and students undergo ability-development curricula. Public everyday school life coexists with advanced research, political control, and hidden operations.

Judgment is a student disciplinary/public-order organization. Anti-Skill is teacher-centered public security. The Board of Directors governs the city in the relevant era under chairman Aleister Crowley. Covert dark-side groups exist, but ordinary students do not have unrestricted knowledge of them.

## Esper system
Students are classified Level 0 through Level 5; there are seven Level 5s in the canon baseline. Ranking is not merely a simple combat-strength list. AIM diffusion fields are faint fields unconsciously generated by espers. Personal Reality is a science-side explanatory framework for ability development.

## Science and magic
The setting contains a science side and a magic side. Major Christian powers include the Anglican, Roman Orthodox, and Russian Orthodox churches. Necessarius is an Anglican combat/occult organization. Ordinary Academy City students do not automatically understand real magic, and magicians do not automatically know Academy City research secrets.

## Touma / Imagine Breaker
Kamijou Touma is measured Level 0. His right hand contains Imagine Breaker, which negates supernatural phenomena on contact across science/magic categories. It does not make him omniscient or physically invulnerable.

## Index
Index is a Necessarius nun with perfect memory who memorizes 103,000 grimoires. Her magic/religion/language expertise is enormous; her science/technology literacy is poor. Everyday hunger and impulsiveness coexist with deep occult knowledge.

## Misaka Mikoto
Mikoto is a 14-year-old Tokiwadai student, Level 5 rank #3, Electromaster/Railgun. She is direct, competitive, action-oriented and justice-minded, with private affection for cute mascot goods. Do not reduce her to one romance trope.

## Tokiwadai / Shokuhou
Tokiwadai is an elite girls' school whose students are Level 3 or above in official Railgun material. Shokuhou Misaki is Level 5 rank #5, controls Mental Out, and leads the school's largest faction as its 'Queen'.

## Accelerator
Level 5 rank #1, vector manipulation and enormous calculation ability. The Sisters/Level 6 Shift history and Last Order matter by this era. Do not spawn him as a generic boss/cameo without location, motive, and current circumstances.

## Daihaseisai anchor
World IF v1 begins September 19 during the giant Academy City athletic festival, with outside visitors admitted. Touma's school and Tokiwadai participate; Touma and Mikoto have a competitive promise tied to results. Canon contains hidden operations behind the public festival, but they are background pressures rather than mandatory checkpoints.

The app uses 08:00 as a convenient seed clock; this is not a claim of an official universal start time.

## Location discipline
Academy City districts have different functions. Respect travel and locality; do not teleport popular characters into scenes without a mechanism.

## Canon vs divergence
Canon is the starting map. Once USER intervention changes a prerequisite, record the divergence, preserve it, update only characters who witnessed/learned it, and recalculate downstream causes instead of restoring the original result by coincidence.
